The Roastery

A small roastery,
a warm room,
a slower morning.

We began with one drum roaster and a stubborn belief that coffee should taste of where it grew. Twelve years later, we still roast in small batches in the back room, twice a week.

Everything you eat is made here before opening — the bread, the pastry, the granola, the jams. Nothing arrives frozen, and nothing is thrown together at the last minute.

  • 01Small-batch roasting twice weekly, in-house
  • 02Pastry and bread baked fresh before opening
  • 03Direct-trade beans from growers we know by name
