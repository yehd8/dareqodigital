The House

A short menu,
a long evening,
a quiet kitchen.

We seat thirty-four guests a night and write the menu each morning around what arrives from the growers and fishermen we have worked with for years.

There is no à la carte rush here. Courses arrive when they are ready, the bread is baked twice a day, and the cellar is open for you to walk through with us before you choose.
