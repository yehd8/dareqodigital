The House

A small bench,
a patient nose,
a signature.

We blend on a single bench in the back of the shop, in batches of no more than two hundred bottles. The materials are chosen for how they behave on warm skin, not how they read on paper.

Every fragrance is aged before it is bottled, and every customer is invited to sit with us, smell slowly, and leave with something that belongs to them — not to the season.

The Composition

Inside a Scent

Every fragrance is built in three movements. Choose one to see the materials we work with.

Bergamot

Calabria

Cold-pressed peel — bright, slightly bitter, and the first thing you notice.

Pink Pepper

Madagascar

A dry, rosy spark that lifts the opening without sweetness.

Neroli

Tunisia

Steam-distilled orange blossom — green, honeyed and quietly luminous.House

Green Mandarin

Sicily

Unripe fruit, sharp and sappy. It disappears within the hour, by design.

Iris

Florence

Root aged three years before use — powdery, cool and faintly metallic.Signature

Jasmine Absolute

Grasse

Picked before sunrise, when the flower gives the most of itself.

Rose de Mai

Grasse

Five weeks a year of harvest. Jammy, green and almost edible.

Orris Butter

Tuscany

The soft, suede-like centre of our No. 01 and the reason it lasts.

Oud

Assam

Aged resin — leather, smoke and damp wood. Used sparingly, always.

Sandalwood

Mysore

Creamy and milky, from sustainably farmed plantations only.

Tonka Bean

Venezuela

Almond, vanilla and a faint hay note that rounds the dry-down.

Ambroxan

Ethical

A modern, cruelty-free alternative to ambergris — warm and skin-like.

Full ingredient and allergen lists are available on request. Concentrations vary by composition.
