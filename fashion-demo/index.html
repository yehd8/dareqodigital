The House

A wardrobe
built slowly,
kept for years.

We buy in small numbers from makers we have known for decades. Nothing here is designed to be replaced next season — only to be worn until it feels like yours.

Our stylists work by appointment, in a quiet room with natural light, so the fitting is never rushed and the advice is never borrowed from a trend report.
