FAQ

A few common questions.

Can I request a quote through the website?

Yes. The request can be submitted for manual review rather than instantly confirmed.

Does the Business Manager automatically confirm customers?

No. The intended workflow lets you review the request first and contact the customer yourself.

Can the website support Arabic?

Yes. The site can use English, Arabic RTL, and Lebanese-style Arabizi modes.

Is the Business Manager production-ready?

This public page presents the concept/demo. A production version requires a secure backend, authentication, database, tenant isolation, backups and monitoring before handling real client data.
